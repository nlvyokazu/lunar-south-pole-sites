Lunar south pole site comparison

This page compares three lunar south pole sites over a date range you choose. For each site it shows how much of the time the Sun and the Earth are above the local terrain horizon, the longest dark and Earth-out periods, and the time to the next sunset and Earthset. It then checks each site against thresholds you set. Geometry only: there is no power, thermal or antenna model. Limits and method.
